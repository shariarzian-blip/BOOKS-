# Stardust Remembering: shot prompts

Shot-by-shot prompts for generating the visuals with an AI image or video tool. Shot numbers match [script.md](script.md).

## Style guide

Paste this at the end of every prompt so the shots look like one film.

```
Cinematic documentary photography, 16:9, photorealistic, natural film grain,
deep blacks, restrained colour palette of indigo, warm gold and soft teal,
slow deliberate camera movement, shallow depth of field where people appear,
no text, no captions, no logos, no watermarks.
```

Rules that keep the result consistent:

- **One movement per shot.** A slow push in, a slow pull back or a slow rise. Never two at once.
- **Length.** Generate 8 to 10 seconds per shot and trim in the edit. Slow shots hide artefacts better than fast ones.
- **People.** Show them from behind, in silhouette or in extreme close-up. No one looks at the camera. Mix ages and cultures across the montage.
- **Space imagery.** Keep it believable, like a real telescope image. No lens flares, no neon, no fantasy nebulae with faces or figures in them.
- **Text.** Add the title card and end card in the edit, never in the generator.

## Narration and music

- **Voice.** Record or generate the narration first and cut the pictures to it. Aim for about 135 words per minute. Leave every `[pause]` in.
- **Music brief.** "Slow ambient score for a contemplative space documentary. Sustained low drone in D, soft bowed strings, a single tanpura tone and occasional singing bowl. No drums, no melody that draws attention. Ten minutes, with a gentle build between minutes six and eight and a quiet resolution at the end."
- **Silence.** The five seconds of silence in Chapter 6 are the centre of the film. Do not fill them.
